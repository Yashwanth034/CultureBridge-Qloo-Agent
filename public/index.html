    THE IDEA BEHIND THE ENGINE

    Not another recommendation list.
    A way to meet in the middle.

    01
    ◎

    Learn both sides

    Each group's favorites are matched to real Qloo entities. Ambiguous matches are surfaced so you're never silently given fabricated taste IDs.

    02
    ⌘

    Investigate separately

    Independent Qloo Insights requests reveal each group's ranked tastes. A third request scouts the joint taste signal.

    03
    ✳

    Mediate fairly

    We reward candidates both sides receive and penalize one-sided picks. Every score has inspectable source ranks, not a made-up AI probability.
